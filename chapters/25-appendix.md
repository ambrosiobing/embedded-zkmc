## What is not claimed

No chapter claims a measured time on any board. No chapter claims that a scheme is secure beyond what its cited paper proves, and where this volume states a theorem it is either the paper's with its number, a textbook result with its citation, or a short lemma proved in full on the page. No chapter claims that a language model contributes soundness; each model chapter carries the theorem that says a wrong proposal is rejected by the certifier. No chapter claims that the KZG setup is trusted: each one that uses it says that the powers of $\tau$ come from a ceremony the bench cannot run and marks the setup as a public parameter it downloads. The HTML edition's mathematics and diagrams are rendered by MathJax and Mermaid loaded from a CDN; without a network the reader sees TeX and Mermaid source as text, which is stated in the colophon.

The second edition adds one more thing not claimed. The design brief this edition drew on \[[93](#references)\] describes its twenty sketches as though the hardware were in hand and the figures were in evidence. They are neither here. Ten of its hardware claims do not hold on this bench and are corrected in the chapters that use them, the retired accelerometer and the eight-billion-parameter model on a 512 MB board being the two that change a design rather than a sentence. Nothing in this volume was taken from that brief as a measurement, and no variant is reported as having been run. A variant is a route the chapter did not take, written down so that the reader can price it, and that is all it is.
